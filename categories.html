Waste Categories

Learn how to separate waste and protect the environment.

Wet Waste

Examples: Fruit peels, vegetable waste and food scraps.

Tip: Keep food waste separate. Compost it where suitable.

Dry Waste

Examples: Paper, cardboard and clean plastic packaging.

Tip: Keep recyclable materials clean and dry.

E-Waste

Examples: Old phones, batteries and electronic devices.

Tip: Take e-waste to an authorised collection point.
