Eco-Friendly Tips

🛍️ Use Reusable Bags

Carry a cloth bag instead of taking a new bag every time.

💧 Use a Refillable Bottle

Use a reusable bottle where safe drinking water is available.

♻️ Separate Waste

Keep different types of waste separate according to local rules.

🔄 Reuse Old Items

Repair, donate or reuse items when possible.

♻️ Recycle Correctly

Keep accepted recyclable materials clean and dry.

💻 Dispose of E-Waste Safely

Take old electronics and batteries to suitable collection centres.
